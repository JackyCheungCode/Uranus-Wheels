# BMW M4 Guide — Source Register and Data Notes

Revision: 2026-11-08  
Scope: F82 / F83 (2014–2020) and G82 / G83 (2021–present).  
Purpose: editorial traceability for the public BMW M4 guide. This register is not a substitute for a vehicle-specific engineering approval.

## Evidence rules

- BMW technical publications are primary evidence for factory engine, output, factory tyre and wheel package data.
- FMB Wheels, PG Wheels, Wheelssize and 4Wheels are secondary fitment references. Their aftermarket examples are displayed as documented examples, not universal approval.
- Market-specific horsepower ratings are kept in their original rating context (PS / hp, US / regional specification).
- PCD, centre bore, fastener and wheel torque must be checked against the exact market / build documentation before a wheel order.
- Brake clearance is wheel-design-specific. Diameter and offset alone do not prove spoke-to-caliper clearance.
- xDrive staggered tyre combinations require a rolling-circumference check against BMW's requirements.
- If a value cannot be tied to a source and an exact scope, it must remain unpublished.

## Known limitations and next verification

1. F82/F83 wheel hardware and factory wheel offsets currently use a secondary fitment guide; cross-check against the specific vehicle's original wheel part number and market.
2. G82/G83 PCD and centre bore are cross-checked against multiple secondary fitment sources; the vehicle-specific parts catalogue / workshop documentation remains the final authority.
3. The 2025+ M4 CS / 2023 M4 CSL wheel width and tyre dimensions are published by BMW. ET values are not inferred where the BMW source does not publish them.
4. Aftermarket setup cards are examples reported by a vendor / community. Before publishing as a Uranus Wheels approved package, record the exact vehicle, brake option, suspension, ride height, alignment, tyre make/model, measured section width, wheel drawing, brake template and test-fit evidence.
5. Image source licences permit reuse under the listed conditions; keep attribution and ShareAlike obligations where applicable.
